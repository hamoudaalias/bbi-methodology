## Rule 2 — Temporal diversity can beat counterfactual density

> Seeing 600 rich consecutive states can teach more than observing 150 states
> under 3 actions each — when the target variable has a **temporal** signature.

**Generating fact** (idem) : `h` manifests itself through the dynamics (E2
ambiguous, E1 delayed) ; the natural sequence brings more information than the
local counterfactual repetition.

**Application** : the choice of the collection mode must be motivated by the
**nature of the signal to be learned** (temporal vs interventional), not by a
priori principles.

## Rule 3 — The H0 protocol is a ground truth, not a training strategy

> The H0 protocol serves to establish the causal truth. It does not constitute,
> by default, the best collection policy for training W.

**Generating fact** (idem, + official CP formulation) : uniform collection is
the **default** training method on this toy ; H0 (a/a_null probing) remains
reserved for the **measure** of v_c_true.

**Application** : systematically dissociate (a) ground-truth collection / causal
evaluation — H0, frozen — and (b) W training collection — choice motivated by
the signal, documented by spec.

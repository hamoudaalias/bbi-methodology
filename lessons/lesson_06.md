## Rule 6 — A low-sample smoke test can produce false positives

**Generating fact** ([[Report Phase 0]] §7) : the 2-episode smoke left the
impression "BBI > PPO on surge" — refuted at 10 seeds (PPO significantly
better, p=0.027).

**Application** : no conclusion without the complete protocol (N paired seeds,
95 % CI, Wilcoxon AND t-test). The smoke serves only the plumbing.

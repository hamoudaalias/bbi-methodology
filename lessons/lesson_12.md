## Rule 12 — A correct sign with compressed amplitude is not a partial success

> Direction without magnitude does not allow **planning** : a planner needs to
> compare effect magnitudes to choose between actions. A predictor that says
> "the right direction" but underestimates the effect by a factor 5-7 is
> unusable for decisions.

**Generating fact** ([[Report W-Diff]], [[Report Gate 0 CityFlow]]) : the
differential loss corrects the **direction** (W-Diff toy : −0.456 vs −4.357) but
the amplitude remains ~10-18 % on both benches. Amplitude failure = gate failure,
whatever the encoding quality (M1 = 73.8-82.5 % ✅) and the sign correctness.

**Application** : an interaction criterion **always** has three legs — sign **and**
amplitude **and** correlation (or another magnitude-fidelity measure). Never
credit a "qualitative progress" of direction as an acceptable partial success :
decision requires amplitude. To address a cumulative interaction, the **learning
target** (not just the evaluation) must carry the magnitude over the horizon
(cf. Design W-HORIZON).

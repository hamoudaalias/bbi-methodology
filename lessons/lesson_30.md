## Lesson 30 — The abstraction level decides the transfer : the same pair of domains can be analogous at one level and radically different at another

(Phase 11 — Chantiers 1-4 + J4, 2026-09-08, verdict PARTIAL, 317 PASS / 0 FAIL)

**Lesson :** *"are two domains analogous?" has no absolute answer — it depends
on the ABSTRACTION LEVEL at which one compares. The intersection and the
oscillator are analogous at the level of CONSTANT EFFECTS (Phase 10 : bias-only
class transfer, ×2 gain) and radically different at the level of ACTION RULES
(Phase 11 : the oscillator has no reducible accumulation — refusal). Neither
answer is wrong ; each transfers exactly what its level can guarantee — and
nothing more.*

Three measured facts underpinning the lesson :

1. **Structure carries the gain, not the data** : on the queues (C/D), the
   PHYSICAL re-verification of A's invariants (action rule + cyclic service)
   gives 0.89 analogy ; the transfer of the regime-conditioned class (the effect
   depends on the observable regime marker, zero coupling to magnitudes) reaches
   the target in ~4-8 pairs where the full affine does not make it within the
   window. On D, cold is STRUCTURALLY poisoned (collinear one-hot + Poisson noise
   on magnitudes : 0.054 fidelity at 160 pairs, saturation) — transfer is not an
   accelerator, it is the only way to learn.

2. **Honest refusal works in BOTH directions** : the radical (the action
   AMPLIFIES the accumulation) is refused at 0.33 — the action rule fails
   re-verification ; and the oscillator is refused at 0.11 at Level 2 even though
   Phase 10 had legitimately transferred it at the effects level. Testing the
   radical BEFORE transferring the class avoided the mirage : bias-only on
   D-regimes would have failed where the radical fails.

3. **The literal ×5 is not measurable in K-ratios when physical ceilings bite** :
   the absolute targets (0.70 fidelity in 1-step) are bounded by the Poisson
   variance of arrivals (ceiling ~0.44-0.49 on C/D) ; the honest target is
   RELATIVE (0.80×ceiling), and the gain then depends on the number of regimes to
   cover (2 on C → K_warm≈4 ; 3 on D → K_warm≈8). The gain caps at ~4× measurable —
   reported as such.

**Warning signal :** a transfer library that does not state ITS abstraction level
(effects? action rules? physical invariants?) — and an analogy computed on
extraction alone without physical re-verification on the target (an extractor can
miss a pattern ; the replayed test does not lie).

**Application :** the ACH layer (Level 1 patterns → re-verifiable Level 2
invariants → analogy weighted by causal relevance) is the mechanism : it
authorizes, refuses or declares the uncertain BEFORE mooring, and the hypothesis
class it transfers (regime-conditioned or bias-only) is exactly what the
invariants guarantee. **Corpus brought to 30 lessons.**

## Lesson 37 — A hidden variable living in time is inferred by temporal dissection, not by impulse : the Causal Touch 'friction' (kick + coast + energy budget) is Lesson 34 generalized to dissipation, and its belief makes manipulation robust to model-mismatch

(GENESIS Phase Gamma — Damped Pendulum with Hidden Friction, 2026-09-09,
VERDICT POSITIVE, 426 PASS / 5 FAIL assumed — Lesson engraved by the carrier
at Gamma close, discovery of the phase)

**Lesson (frozen formulation) :** *"When the hidden parameter is null at rest
(viscous friction is only seen if one creates the velocity), the causal
intervention must be composed in time — kick to activate, coast to dissipate —
and the inference is done by energy budget, a closed deterministic formula. A
belief inferred this way (≤ 3 %) feeds W as-is and makes equilibrium
manipulation robust even under ±20 % model-mismatch."*

**Contexts :** viscous friction `b = 0 at equilibrium` : no impulsive torque
reveals it — even symmetric. Response : 2 composed H0 pairs (±5 N·m, h=4 kick →
k=250 coast), measurement of the energy `E = ½mL²ω² + mgL(1−cosθ)`, budget
`dE/dt = −b·ω²` ⇒ `b_est = (E0−E1)/(Σω²dt)`.

**Measurements (T_gamma_3..4, 30/30 seeds 82020..82049) :**

1. **The discrete coast carries a systematic bias 2 %·b, reproduced as-is**
   (error 0.99 % / 1.99 % / 3.00 % for b=0.5/1.0/1.5 ; identical to the J0
   diligence measured BEFORE sealing). The sealed formula is never calibrated :
   the rounding bias of the discrete budget is documented and counts as
   **testimony** — the closed inference is exact for any amplitude, the discrete
   one is not quite ; a systematic 2 %·b is an honest signature, not a defect to
   fix in secret.
2. **W/C horizon coherence is a prerequisite of fidelity** : a first setup
   measured W prediction over 2 steps but the counterfactual C over 1 step
   (factor 2, fidelity 0.16). Committing the action over the σ′ span (sealed §9)
   brings fidelity back to **0.9992–0.9999** — *W and C must look at the same
   duration, otherwise "fidelity" measures the horizon incoherence, not the
   quality of the seen world* (method lesson, core intact).
3. **The friction belief makes manipulation robust to model-mismatch** :
   θ_target=0.30 (tenable : τ_hold=2.8997, margin 42 %), TERMINAL criterion
   |θ(100 steps)−θ_c| < 0.10, worst error **0.0026 rad** 20/20, **identical under
   b_agent × ±20 %** (60/60) — at rest the band is not reached (dist 0.30), so
   the manipulation earns its energy, and the doubt about the dissipator changes
   nothing.

**Application :** 1) before "touching" a hidden parameter, ask *where it
lives* : in an instantaneous coupling (mass → impulse) or in a time constant
(friction → kick + coast + budget) — the same Causal-Touch line does not serve
both ; 2) export the discretization biases of the budget as measured, never
recalibrated — they are the honest signature of the integrator ; 3) fix the
horizon of W AND C to the σ′ span before judging fidelity ; 4) manipulation
robustness to ±20 % of belief is live proof that causal inference (≤ 3 %) is
*abundant* for the task — plausible model-mismatch does not break the control.

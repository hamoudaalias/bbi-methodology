## Lesson 40 — In visual perception, the W/C fidelity margin is structurally thinner than in direct continuous state : a threshold calibrated on a diligence allocation may not hold on a different eval allocation, even if the loop does better than the sealed reference on that allocation — the perceptual information loss must be provisioned in the threshold, not discovered at J3

(Extension Perception Visuelle — J3, C4 W/C fidelity, 2026-09-09, VERDICT
NEGATIVE, C4 0.8958 < 0.9, cut-off triggered, Lesson engraved by the carrier at
J4 close, Chef decision)

**Lesson (frozen formulation) :** *"W/C fidelity in visual perception has a
structurally thinner margin than in direct continuous state. A threshold
calibrated on a diligence allocation may not hold on a different eval allocation,
even if the loop does better than the sealed reference on that allocation. Visual
perception introduces an irreducible information loss that must be provisioned in
the threshold, not discovered at J3."*

**Contexts :** J0' had calibrated CV4 on the diligence family 85035..85054
(0.9177, margins +0.0177 above the 0.9 threshold). The Chef allocation for the J3
loop (85028..85047) contains 8 massive seeds (m ≈ 2.5–4.7) whose bang-bang ±5
control sustains high |ω̂| — outside the linear corridor where the W core is exact.
The ω̂ channel of the 64×64 E codec (under-quantization |Δθ| < 0.002 rad,
declared `missing ['omega']` from J1) leaves a residual that exceeds the agreement
tolerance (15 % of max(|measured|, 0.02)) on the angular-velocity difference.
Result : 0.8958 (< 0.9), gap −0.42 %.

**Measurements (2000 cycles, 20 seeds × 100, digest `be662f1c…` byte-stable 2 runs) :**

1. **The loop does better than the sealed reference on its own range** :
   CV4 reference (strict diligence (2,3,4) protocol) on 85028..85047 =
   **0.8653** < measured loop **0.8958** — the E-W-C-P-L integration improved
   the fidelity ; the threshold, for its part, remained calibrated on the
   diligence allocation.
2. **The fidelity splits into two explainable clusters** : calm / coast-dominant
   seeds (τ=0 ≥ 50 %) → **0.91–1.00** ; massive seeds + sustained bang-bang ±5 →
   **0.65–0.88**. The loss correlates with the dynamics, not the code.
3. **None of the "hard negative" §7 cut-offs is triggered** : determinism
   20/20, H0 OK, Causal Touch OK, stable loop 0 crash/0 NaN/0 over_budget, 0
   oracle leak — the failure is a **value** under the objective, not a mechanism.
4. **The cause was anticipated (Chef GO J3 vigilance)** : "if fidelity drops
   under 0.9, it is a signal that E or the temporal chaining of Δz is
   insufficient." The signal materialized → relaunch lever, not ceiling.

**Application :** 1) provision the perceptual loss in the threshold FROM the
sealing (the threshold of a perception chain must be calibrated on the sealed
reference of the range, not on a neighboring diligence allocation) ; 2)
scientific audit : a NEGATIVE at −0.42 % with the same-range sealed reference
lower is a real but honestly documented failure — never masked, never moved
(Lesson 30) ; 3) dig the limiting channel (codec ω̂) prioritizing encoder E
improvement (relaunch Option B) before any recalibration ; 4) any relaunch goes
through a new sealed J0 (never a modification of the sealed spec).

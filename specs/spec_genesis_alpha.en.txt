GENESIS Phase Alpha — SEALED Spec of the Damped Pendulum (sealed J0 §2)
==================================================================

PHYSICAL SYSTEM
  Simple damped pendulum with hidden mass, 1 degree of freedom.
  State   : [θ, ω]        θ ∈ [-π, π], ω ∈ ℝ
  L = 1.0 m              arm length (fixed)
  g = 9.81 m/s²          gravity (fixed)
  m ∈ [0.5, 5.0] kg      mass (HIDDEN VARIABLE h)
  b ∈ [0.1, 2.0]         viscous friction (HIDDEN VARIABLE h)
  Equation (Lagrange) : θ'' = -(g/L)·sin(θ) - (b/m)·ω + τ/(m·L²)

ACTIONS (discrete, MultiStepPlanner-compatible)
  a ∈ {0, 1, 2, 3, 4} → τ ∈ {-5.0, -2.5, 0.0, 2.5, 5.0} N·m
  a_probe = 3 (τ = +2.5 N·m), a_null = 2 (τ = 0.0)  [Causal Touch §4]

INTEGRATION
  Home-made Runge-Kutta 4 (no scipy.integrate), dt = 0.02 s.
  float64 everywhere; no non-deterministic operation.

OBSERVATION
  Full state [θ, ω] (no velocity inference required at J1).

COST
  total = (θ - θ_target)² + λ·τ²,  λ = 0.01, θ_target = π/2.
  cumulative energy tracked: Σ τ² per step.

FROZEN CONSTANTS (module genesis_arm_env.py)
  L=1.0 · G=9.81 · DT=0.02 · N_ACTIONS=5
  TAU=(-5.0,-2.5,0.0,2.5,5.0) · M_RANGE=(0.5,5.0) · B_RANGE=(0.1,2.0)
  LAMBDA=0.01 · THETA_TARGET=π/2

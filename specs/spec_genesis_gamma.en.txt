5. CAUSAL TOUCH GAMMA (FROZEN) — compound kick + coast protocol
==================================================================
FRICTION IS ZERO AT REST: an impulse from rest creates velocity
(reveals inertia m·L², KNOWN), but friction acts ONLY during motion.
The Touch must therefore be COMPOUND:
    1) KICK: create velocity (ω ≠ 0) via a torque applied for h steps;
    2) COAST: observe the decay of ω under τ = 0 for k steps;
    3) INFER: b from the dissipation energy balance.

PROTOCOL (2 H0 pairs per seed — to observe decay, velocity must have
been created; 2 amplitudes to separate b from any amplitude dependence
and verify viscous friction symmetry):
    rest [0,0]/[0,0] → snapshot →
    Pair 1: probe = kick τ=+5.0 N·m (a=4), h=4 steps, then coast τ=0
              (a=2 NULL) k=250 steps       → b_est⁽¹⁾
              null   = coast τ=0, k=250 steps from rest (no
                       motion, no dissipation to observe)
    Pair 2: probe = kick τ=-5.0 N·m (a=0), h=4 steps, then coast τ=0
              (a=2 NULL) k=250 steps       → b_est⁽²⁾
              null   = identical (degenerate: rest remains rest)
    b_agent = (b_est⁽¹⁾ + b_est⁽²⁾)/2
    AGREEMENT (audit T_gamma_3d): |b_est⁽¹⁾ − b_est⁽²⁾| = 0 (friction
    symmetry: dissipation does not depend on the sign of ω).

INFERENCE FORMULA — ENERGY BALANCE (m, L, g known):
    E(θ, ω) = ½·m·L²·ω² + m·g·L·(1 - cos θ)
    During coast (τ = 0), only friction dissipates:
    dE/dt = -b·ω²  ⇒  b_est = (E0 - E1) / ( Σ_{j} ω_j² · dt )  ,
    where E0 = E(θ,ω) at coast start, E1 = E(θ,ω) at coast end,
    Σ_j ω_j²·dt over the k coast steps (observed samples).

DECAY EQUATIONS (diagnostic, linearized small-θ · τ=0):
    θ̈ + (b/m)·θ̇ + (g/L)·θ = 0
    ω_n = √(g/L) = 3.132 rad/s ;  ζ = b/(2·m·ω_n) ∈ [0.016, 0.32]
    (underdamped across the entire domain);
    exponential velocity envelope e^{-ζ·ω_n·t};
    logarithmic decrement Λ = ln(ω_+/ω_-) ≈ ζ·ω_n·T (diagnostic).
The energy balance (exact for all θ, independent of damping)
is the DECLARED FORMULA; the logarithmic decrement is a diagnostic.

MEASURED (discrete RK4 world, J0 diligence): b_est error 0.99 % /
1.99 % / 3.00 % for b ∈ {0.5, 1.0, 1.5} (systematic bias ~2·b %
